Hi, I’m Agastya Kommanamanchi, a forward-deployed engineer who turns ambitious AI, data and cloud priorities into secure systems that ship. For more than a decade, I’ve built enterprise platforms—from recommendations serving more than 50 million users to generative AI workflows achieving over 95 percent task success. I love forward-deployed engineering because it keeps me close to the real problem. I sit with users, understand the workflow, make architecture tangible and stay accountable from prototype through production and adoption. I bring hands-on engineering, product judgment and executive communication. If you’re building high-impact enterprise AI, I’d love to help turn the idea into a measurable outcome.
